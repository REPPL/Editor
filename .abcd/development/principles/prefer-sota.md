# Prefer SOTA, adversary-filtered

## The convention

Where a question has a credible state-of-the-art answer, that answer is the
presumptive pick. It is not the automatic one. Before it is adopted it is
challenged for fit against this repository's own preferences, and the challenge
is recorded whichever way it goes.

## Why it earns its keep

The alternative to a presumption is a fresh argument every time, and a fresh
argument is decided by whoever is most fluent rather than by what is true. A
presumption puts the burden of proof in the right place: the person departing
from the state of the art has to say why, in writing, and the person following
it does not have to re-derive it.

The adversary half exists because "state of the art" is a claim about a field,
not about this repository. A field optimising for team scale, for hiring, or
for cross-platform reach will reach answers a single-maintainer, macOS-first,
keyboard-first Markdown editor should decline. The presumption survives a
challenge or it does not; either outcome is a finding.

## What it costs

A survey before a decision, and a record of the survey afterwards. For a small
choice that is more ceremony than the choice deserves, which is why the
convention applies to choices that are hard to reverse, not to every one.

## How to tell it is being followed

- A tool, pattern or convention adopted without any statement of what the
  alternatives were is a presumption never tested.
- A departure from a widely held practice with no recorded reason is the
  failure this convention exists to catch, whichever direction it points.
- A research note that ends in a ranked recommendation with evidence tiers, and
  a decision record that cites it, is the shape of this working.
